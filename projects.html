LLM Prompt Optimization via Reward Feedback

1/2020 – 5/2020

Built a lightweight reward-guided framework for improving language-model outputs through candidate generation and selection.

  • Simulated reinforcement-learning concepts such as policy evaluation and policy improvement through iterative reward-based selection.

Technologies: Python, PyTorch, LLMs, Reward Functions, Reinforcement Learning
