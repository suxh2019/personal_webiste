Robust Reinforcement Learning for Sim-to-Real Control

3/2026 – 4/2026

Developed reinforcement learning policies for continuous-control tasks with an emphasis on robustness to simulation-to-real-world mismatch.

  • Trained continuous-control RL policies in simulated environments using PyTorch.
  • Modeled sim-to-real gaps through structured variations in system dynamics and environment parameters.
  • Applied domain randomization to improve policy robustness across changing dynamics.
  • Evaluated policy performance under perturbed environments to study generalization and transfer robustness.

Technologies: PyTorch, Reinforcement Learning, Continuous Control, Domain Randomization, Sim-to-Real
